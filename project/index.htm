This project began to take shape in 2025.



I owned my first 3D printer in 2013, and over the years I printed many different items.
Some were designed from scratch using 3D modelling software, while others were downloaded from platforms such as Thingiverse and then used at home or sold.

I also started a small 3D printing hub, where I produced prototypes
and custom-made items on demand, mainly using ABS and PLA.
Over time, my focus evolved toward a deeper concern for plastic waste:
how it is collected, processed, reduced, and reused.
I feel the need to participate more actively in the circular economy and to become part of the solution.

The main idea behind this project is to use 3D printing as a development tool
for products that can later be manufactured from recycled plastic pellets.
These products should be useful, durable, and designed with their full life cycle in mind —
including the possibility of being reused, recycled,
or reprocessed when they reach the end of their life.

From concept to product development,
the goal is to create practical and innovative items that are not always easy to find on the market,
or that can be improved through better design and material choices.
Another important aspect of the project is to use European recycled plastic pellets whenever possible.
This helps support local and regional recycling industries
while reducing dependence on virgin plastic materials.

The products can also be easily personalised, customised, or branded,
making them suitable for private customers, companies, events, sponsors, and promotional use.
